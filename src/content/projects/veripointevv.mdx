## 🏛️ The Problem: Federal Mandates & SaaS Friction

Section 12006 of the federal **21st Century Cures Act** mandates that all state Medicaid programs implement Electronic Visit Verification for Personal Care Services (PCS) and Home Health Care Services. Every submitted visit must verify 6 statutory data points:

1. **Type of service performed** (HCPCS billing code)
2. **Individual receiving the service** (Medicaid recipient ID)
3. **Date of the service**
4. **Location of service delivery** (GPS coordinates & geofence attestation)
5. **Individual providing the service** (Caregiver / Attendant ID or NPI)
6. **Time service begins and ends** (Clock-in and clock-out timestamps)

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short
Mainstream commercial EVV aggregators and SaaS vendors (Netsmart, Sandata, HHAeXchange, Tellus) were architected around large enterprise agencies:
* **Per-Seat Licensing & Cloud Lock-in:** Expensive per-caregiver monthly SaaS subscription fees that strain independent providers and small agencies.
* **Fragile Internet Dependencies:** Cloud-first mobile apps fail or discard logs in rural areas or during network blackouts.
* **Ignoring Self-Directed Care:** Most software assumes a multi-client dispatch agency model, creating immense friction for consumer-directed Medicaid programs where an individual attendant works 1-on-1 with a single client.

VeriPoint EVV was engineered around a decoupled, native 3-tier architecture operating without cloud dependencies:

### Key Technical Differentiators

### 1. Dual Architecture (Self-Directed & Traditional Agency)
* **Dedicated Attendant Pairing:** Built-in auto-pairing logic matches consumer-directed attendants 1-on-1 with their designated clients (such as Montana **Community First Choice `T1019 U9`** or **Big Sky Waiver `S5125 UA`**).
* **Agency Dispatch Mode:** Supports traditional agency operations with floating staff, shift scheduling, and conflict detection.

### 2. Integrated Point-of-Care Transit Mileage (HCPCS A0425)
* Real-time odometer tracking during medical escort (`T2001 U9`), grocery shopping, and community integration (`S5126 U9`).
* Auto-calculates travel reimbursements initialized to the federal IRS benchmark (**$0.76/mile**) with support for state-specific Non-Emergency Medical Transit (NEMT) fee schedules.

### 3. Cryptographically Sealed Dual Digital Signatures
* Interactive touchscreen/mouse signature canvas capturing **both** caregiver and client digital signatures at clock-out.
* Includes timestamp attestation, GPS capture, and tamper-evident SHA-256 hashes (`VeriPointSig_...`) ensuring immutable audit evidence.

### 4. True Offline-First Reliability
* Local SQLite storage (`evv.db`) and local encrypted JSON data stores ensure shifts, GPS locations, and signatures are recorded without internet connectivity.
* Synchronizes cleanly with state aggregators once a connection is re-established.

### 5. Multi-State Aggregator Engine
* Dynamic State Configuration Profiles (`state_profiles.json`) allow agencies to switch between state Medicaid aggregators without refactoring:
  * **Montana (MT):** DPHHS SLTC Division — Netsmart (Mobile Caregiver+) Pipe-Delimited CSV / REST Alt-EVV.
  * **Texas (TX):** HHSC LTSS — HHAeXchange Alt-EVV EDI / API.
  * **Ohio (OH) & Pennsylvania (PA):** Sandata Alt-EVV REST.
  * **ANSI X12 837P:** Professional electronic claim generator with CMS 8-minute rounding rules (15-minute unit increments).

## 🛠️ Stack & Technologies

* **Desktop Application:** Python, Tkinter / TTK styling, SQLite (`evv.db`)
* **Mobile Point-of-Care:** Python (Kivy / Buildozer) & Native Android (Kotlin, `MainActivity.kt`)
* **Billing & EDI Engine:** ANSI X12 837P HIPAA 5010X222A1 generator with CMS 8-minute rounding logic
* **Data & Compliance:** JSON schemas, CSV export engines, SHA-256 cryptographic attestation hashes
